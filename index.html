About

I'm a product designer in Lagos, Nigeria - three years across fintech, SaaS, e-commerce and consumer apps.

Every project starts by listening. Questionnaires, one-on-one interviews, personas. People will tell you exactly what's broken if you ask them properly.

On Stella's Kitchen I designed for users who might not read English - icons, colour and contrast doing the work that text usually does.

I work in Figma next to PMs and front-end developers, hand off through Dev Mode, and judge a design by whether it survives that handoff.

I've spent the last two years building an AI-augmented workflow, and it bought me back the hours that used to go on grunt work. They go into judgement now.
